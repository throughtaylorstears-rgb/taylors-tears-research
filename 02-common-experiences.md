# Common Experiences of Children of Addicted Parents
## What Children Globally Experience Growing Up in Addiction

---

## 🏠 CHILDHOOD EXPERIENCES

### 1. UNPREDICTABILITY & CHAOS
- Never knowing what mood parent will be in
- Walking on eggshells constantly
- Sudden outbursts, violence, or emotional swings
- House feeling "unsafe" at any moment

### 2. PARENTIFICATION
- Taking care of parent when drunk/high
- Taking care of siblings
- Managing household responsibilities
- Being the "responsible one" early in life

### 3. EMOTIONAL NEGLECT
- Parent not emotionally available
- Feelings dismissed ("you're too sensitive")
- Learned to suppress emotions
- "Don't feel, don't talk, don't trust"

### 4. ISOLATION & SECRECY
- Hiding what's happening at home
- Lying to friends, teachers
- Embarrassed to invite friends over
- Feared being taken away by authorities

### 5. WITNESSING TRAUMA
- Seeing fights, police visits
- Experiencing or witnessing abuse (verbal, physical, sexual)
- Parent's withdrawal or overdose
- Divorce, moving, loss of home

### 6. INSTABILITY
- Frequent moves
- Changing schools
- Different households
- Financial instability

### 7. MISSED DEVELOPMENT
- Not having childhood experiences
- Missing school events
- Having to grow up too fast
- Not learning healthy relationship patterns

---

## 💔 EMOTIONAL IMPACT

### Fear & Anxiety
- Constant state of hypervigilance
- "Fawn" response - trying to please everyone
- Can't relax, always on alert
- Fear of abandonment

### Guilt & Shame
- Feeling like it's their fault
- "If I was better..."
- Thinking they could have fixed it
- Carrying family secrets

### Anger & Resentment
- At parent(s) for choices
- At other parent for not protecting them
- At siblings who "don't understand"
- At themselves for feeling angry

### Grief
- Lost "normal" childhood
- Lost the parent they wanted
- Loss of safety and stability
- Grief that continues into adulthood

---

## 👁️ HOW THEY SEE THEMSELVES

- "I'm not enough"
- "I have to earn love"
- "My needs don't matter"
- "I must take care of everyone else first"
- "I can't trust anyone"
- "I'm defective/broken"
- "Normal families don't look like mine"

---

## 🔄 INTERGENERATIONAL PATTERNS

Many children of addicts later:
- Gravitate toward similar relationships
- Repeat patterns (marry addicts, become workaholics)
- Become caregivers/people pleasers
- Struggle with the same addictions
- Have difficulty with their own children
