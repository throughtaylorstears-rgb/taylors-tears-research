# Children's Book Concept #2: "Blue Bunny's Big Feelings"
## Emotional Regulation & Trauma-Informed Children's Book | Canva-Ready Pages

---

## BOOK OVERVIEW

**Character:** Blue Bunny - A soft, round blue bunny (the child's companion/therapy mascot)
**Perspective:** Blue Bunny guiding a child through emotions
**Mood:** Calming, validating, gentle
**Age Range:** 3-7 years old (picture book)
**Purpose:** Helps children name and regulate big emotions, especially in chaotic homes

---

## PAGE-BY-PAGE BREAKDOWN

### PAGE 1: COVER
**Visual:** Blue Bunny sitting in a cozy green meadow, surrounded by wildflowers, looking at the reader with warm eyes
**Text:** "Blue Bunny's Big Feelings"
**Subtitle:** "A story about feelings that feel too big"
**Design Notes:** Soft blue bunny with big gentle eyes, peaceful scene

### PAGE 2: Title Page
**Visual:** Blue Bunny holding a flower, close-up
**Text:** "Blue Bunny's Big Feelings"
**Text:** "By Through Taylor's Tears"
**Design Notes:** Simple, warm

### PAGE 3: Meet Blue Bunny
**Visual:** Blue Bunny popping out of a hollow log, curious and friendly
**Text:** "This is Blue Bunny. Blue Bunny lives in a burrow under the big oak tree. Blue Bunny has soft blue fur and the fluffiest ears. And Blue Bunny knows something important: Sometimes feelings get BIG."
**Design Notes:** Introduce character as friendly, curious

### PAGE 4: The First Feeling - Sad
**Visual:** Blue Bunny with droopy ears, sitting in rain, looking tearful but calm
**Text:** "Sometimes Blue Bunny feels sad. The sad feeling comes when things feel wrong. Blue Bunny's ears droop. Blue Bunny's eyes get wet. And that's okay. Sad is a feeling that comes to visit."
**Design Notes:** Rain shown as soft blue droplets, not stormy

### PAGE 5: The Second Feeling - Mad
**Visual:** Blue Bunny with ears standing up, puffing up like a ball, but still cute
**Text:** "Sometimes Blue Bunny feels mad. The mad feeling comes when something isn't fair. Blue Bunny's ears stand straight up. Blue Bunny wants to stomp and yell. And that's okay too. Mad is a feeling that comes to visit."
**Design Notes:** Make "mad" look powerful but not scary - like a fuzzy ball of energy

### PAGE 6: The Third Feeling - Scared
**Visual:** Blue Bunny in a little burrow, ears tucked, eyes wide but calm
**Text:** "Sometimes Blue Bunny feels scared. The scared feeling comes when things feel dangerous. Blue Bunny's ears go down. Blue Bunny wants to hide. And that's okay. Scared is a feeling that comes to visit."
**Design Notes:** Burrow looks cozy, not like a scary cave

### PAGE 7: The Fourth Feeling - Confused
**Visual:** Blue Bunny with a question mark above head, tilting head
**Text:** "Sometimes Blue Bunny feels confused. The confused feeling comes when things don't make sense. Blue Bunny's brain goes whoosh. Blue Bunny doesn't know what to do. And that's okay. Confused is a feeling that comes to visit."
**Design Notes:** Question mark as a soft cloud shape

### PAGE 8: All the Feelings
**Visual:** Blue Bunny with all feelings shown as gentle colored bubbles around them
**Text:** "Blue Bunny has lots of feelings. Sad, mad, scared, confused, happy, excited, silly, proud... ALL of them are okay. No feeling is too big or too small. Every feeling is welcome here."
**Design Notes:** Rainbow of soft-colored bubbles, Blue Bunny in center smiling

### PAGE 9: What To Do With Big Feelings
**Visual:** Blue Bunny doing the "bunny breath" - taking a deep breath, belly rounded
**Text:** "When feelings get too big, Blue Bunny knows a secret. It's called bunny breath. Breathe in slow... breathe out slow... Keep breathing. Your heart will calm down. Your brain will listen again."
**Design Notes:** Show breathing visually - belly expanding, soft blue glow

### PAGE 10: The Feeling Places
**Visual:** Blue Bunny showing where feelings live - heart (love/sad), tummy (worries), chest (mad)
**Text:** "Where do feelings live? They live in your body! In your heart, your tummy, your chest. When you feel something, notice where in your body it lives. That helps you understand the feeling."
**Design Notes:** Simple body diagram with feeling locations marked

### PAGE 11: Finding Help
**Visual:** Blue Bunny with other animal friends - a wise owl, a gentle deer
**Text:** "Blue Bunny doesn't have big feelings alone. Blue Bunny has friends who help. The wise owl listens. The gentle deer gives hugs. You have helpers too. Safe grown-ups who love you."
**Design Notes:** Show community - diverse friendly animals

### PAGE 12: You Are Enough
**Visual:** Blue Bunny looking in a puddle/reflective water, seeing their own reflection smiling
**Text:** "Blue Bunny wants you to know: You are enough exactly right now. You don't have to be different. You don't have to be quieter or louder or better. You are good. You are worthy. You are loved."
**Design Notes:** Warm, mirror/reflection showing happy Blue Bunny

### PAGE 13: For the Grown-Ups
**Visual:** Simple calming page
**Text:** "For the grown-ups: This book helps children identify and regulate emotions. Learn more at through-taylors-tears.com"
**Design Notes:** Clean, minimal

### PAGE 14: Back Cover
**Visual:** Blue Bunny waving goodbye, nestled in flowers
**Text:** "Through Taylor's Tears"
**Tagline:** "Every feeling is welcome here"
**Design Notes:** Warm, inviting

## CANVA SETUP NOTES

- **Page Size:** 8.5 x 11" (standard picture book)
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary: Soft Blue (#A0C4FF)
  - Secondary: Warm Cream (#FFF8E7)
  - Accent: Soft Green (#B8E0D2) for nature
  - Text: Dark Navy (#2D3748)
  - Feeling Colors: Blue (sad), Red-Pink (mad), Purple (scared), Orange (confused)
- **Fonts:**
  - Title: Rounded, soft (Fredoka One, Quicksand)
  - Body: Friendly, readable (Nunito, Open Sans)
- **Art Style:** Soft, rounded digital illustrations - very huggable, cozy aesthetic
