# Children's Book Concept #2: "Blue Bunny's Big Feelings"
## With Rhyming Text

---

## BOOK OVERVIEW

- **Character:** Blue Bunny (soft, round, friendly bunny)
- **Perspective:** Blue Bunny narrator
- **Rhyme Scheme:** AABB, 2-4 lines per spread
- **Age Range:** 3-7 years old

---

## PAGE SPREADS

### SPREAD 1: Cover
**Page 1 (Left):** [Blue Bunny in meadow]
**Page 1 (Right):**
```
BLUE BUNNY'S BIG FEELINGS

A story about feelings that feel too big
```

---

### SPREAD 2: Meet Blue Bunny
**Page 2 (Left):** [Blue Bunny popping out of burrow]
**Page 2 (Right):**
```
This is Blue Bunny, soft fur so blue,
With the fluffiest ears and a nose that smells new,
Blue Bunny lives under the big old oak tree,
And Blue Bunny knows something good to see:
```

---

### SPREAD 3: All the Feelings
**Page 3 (Left):** [All feelings as bubbles]
**Page 3 (Right):**
```
Feelings come visit—the happy, the sad,
The mad and the scared and the excite-y and bad,
They're all just as welcome, they're all here to stay,
Every single feeling is okay!
```

---

### SPREAD 4: Sad Feeling
**Page 4 (Left):** [Blue Bunny with droopy ears in rain]
**Page 4 (Right):**
```
Sometimes Blue Bunny feels super sad,
The cry-y kind of feeling that's really bad,
Tears fall like raindrops from eyes big and round—
But that's okay too; tears help us be sound.
```

---

### SPREAD 5: Mad Feeling
**Page 5 (Left):** [Blue Bunny puffed up, ears up]
**Page 5 (Right):**
```
Sometimes Blue Bunny feels really mad,
Stompy and shouty—that's what I've had,
My ears go right up and I hop in a ring,
But I breathe and I breathe and I breathe in everything.
```

---

### SPREAD 6: Scared Feeling
**Page 6 (Left):** [Blue Bunny in cozy burrow]
**Page 6 (Right):**
```
Sometimes Blue Bunny feels scared of the dark,
The shiver-y kind that makes my heart spark,
I hide in my burrow, I tuck in my feet—
But I know that the morning will soon make it sweet.
```

---

### SPREAD 7: Confused Feeling
**Page 7 (Left):** [Blue Bunny with question marks]
**Page 7 (Right):**
```
Sometimes Blue Bunny feels mixed up inside,
The whoosh-y brain feeling with nowhere to hide,
I don't know what's happening, what's right or what's wrong—
But feelings don't have to be perfect and strong.
```

---

### SPREAD 8: Bunny Breath
**Page 8 (Left):** [Blue Bunny doing breathing exercise]
**Page 8 (Right):**
```
When feelings get big and they fill up my heart,
Blue Bunny knows how to make a fresh start,
Breathe in slow... breathe out slow...
Keep breathing—watch worry let go!
```

---

### SPREAD 9: Feeling Places
**Page 9 (Left):** [Body feeling map]
**Page 9 (Right):**
```
Where do feelings live? Right here in my body!
In my heart, in my tummy, inside there and stony,
When I feel something funny, I notice where—
That helps me know what to do with my care.
```

---

### SPREAD 10: Finding Helpers
**Page 10 (Left):** [Blue Bunny with animal friends]
**Page 10 (Right):**
```
Blue Bunny has helpers—both feathered and fur,
The wise old owl and the gentle young deer,
You have helpers too—look around and you'll see,
Safe grown-ups who love you exactly like me.
```

---

### SPREAD 11: You Are Enough
**Page 11 (Left):** [Blue Bunny looking in water reflection]
**Page 11 (Right):**
```
Blue Bunny wants you to know what's true:
You are enough exactly—yes, right now is you,
You don't have to be bigger or quieter or best,
You are worthy and loved—you are better than blessed!
```

---

### SPREAD 12: Back Cover
**Page 12 (Left):** [Blank]
**Page 12 (Right):**
```
THROUGH TAYLOR'S TEARS

Every feeling is welcome here

© Through Taylor's Tears
```

## CANVA NOTES

- **Page Size:** Two-page spreads
- **Font:** Rounded, soft (Fredoka One, Quicksand)
- **Illustrations:** Soft blue bunny, cozy aesthetic
- **Color Palette:** Soft blue (#A0C4FF), cream (#FFF8E7), green (#B8E0D2)
