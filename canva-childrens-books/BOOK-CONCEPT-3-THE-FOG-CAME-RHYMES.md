# Children's Book Concept #3: "The Fog Came"
## With Rhyming Text

---

## BOOK OVERVIEW

- **Character:** Blue Bunny + The Fog (soft purple/blue mist)
- **Perspective:** Blue Bunny narrator
- **Rhyme Scheme:** AABB, 2-4 lines per spread
- **Age Range:** 4-8 years old

---

## PAGE SPREADS

### SPREAD 1: Cover
**Page 1 (Left):** [Cozy cottage with soft fog rolling in]
**Page 1 (Right):**
```
THE FOG CAME

A gentle story about tricky times at home
```

---

### SPREAD 2: Before the Fog
**Page 2 (Left):** [Sunny cottage, warm and bright]
**Page 2 (Right):**
```
Before The Fog came, the house was so warm,
There was breakfast and laughter and sunshine that form,
The smiles were all plenty, the days felt so true—
Everything felt safe through and through.
```

---

### SPREAD 3: The Fog Arrives
**Page 3 (Left):** [First wisps of fog creeping in]
**Page 3 (Right):**
```
Then one day The Fog came—it crept in so slow,
You couldn't quite see it, but you sure could know,
The house got all quiet, the smiles got all few,
Something felt different—The Fog was here true.
```

---

### SPREAD 4: What The Fog Does
**Page 4 (Left):** [Blue Bunny in foggy house]
**Page 4 (Right):**
```
When The Fog is in the house, things get really wacky,
Grown-ups sleep lots and act super flaky,
They forget about dinner and they forget your name—
But that's not your fault—Fog is tricky to blame.
```

---

### SPREAD 5: Not My Job to Fix
**Page 5 (Left):** [Blue Bunny doing kid things - playing]
**Page 5 (Right):**
```
Kids always wonder, "Can I make them smile?"
"Can I fix The Fog and make everything worthwhile?"
The answer is no—that's their job, not yours,
Your job is to play and to learn and explore!
```

---

### SPREAD 6: My Safe Place
**Page 6 (Left):** [Cozy burrow/safe place]
**Page 6 (Right):**
```
When The Fog is around, find your safe spot in,
For Blue Bunny it's burrows—soft, cozy, and in,
For you it might be your bed with that throw,
Or grandma's house—that's a safe place to go!
```

---

### SPREAD 7: Feelings Under The Fog
**Page 7 (Left):** [Colored lights cutting through fog]
**Page 7 (Right):**
```
Underneath Fog, there are feelings so true,
Sad, mad, scared, confused—all inside you,
The Fog tries to hide them but they're still there,
Your feelings matter—that much is fair!
```

---

### SPREAD 8: Finding Helpers
**Page 8 (Left):** [Blue Bunny with helper adult]
**Page 8 (Right):**
```
You don't have to handle The Fog all alone,
There's helpers all round you—look far, look close to home,
A teacher who listens, a grandma who sees,
Tell a safe person and let them help ease!
```

---

### SPREAD 9: The Fog Goes Away
**Page 9 (Left):** [Fog lifting, sun coming through]
**Page 9 (Right):**
```
Sometimes The Fog goes and the sun finds its way,
The grown-ups wake up and they're happy today,
And you think, "Yay, Foggy's gone!"—but sometimes it returns,
But here's what I know: the sun always burns!
```

---

### SPREAD 10: Even With The Fog
**Page 10 (Left):** [Blue Bunny playing despite fog]
**Page 10 (Right):**
```
Here's the big secret—even when Fog is near,
You can still be happy, you can still play with cheer,
The Fog is so tricky but here's what is true:
You are brave and strong—and that applies to you!
```

---

### SPREAD 11: Back Cover
**Page 11 (Left):** [Blank]
**Page 11 (Right):**
```
THROUGH TAYLOR'S TEARS

You are brave. You are strong.

© Through Taylor's Tears
```

## CANVA NOTES

- **Page Size:** Two-page spreads
- **Font:** Rounded, soft (Fredoka One, Baloo)
- **Illustrations:** Cottage aesthetic, soft purple-blue fog
- **Color Palette:** Soft purple (#B8B5FF), cream (#FFF8E7), golden (#F6D365)
