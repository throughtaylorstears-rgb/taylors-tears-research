## 🎨 Canva Setup (Same for All Books)

- **Page Size:** 8.5 x 11" (creates two-page spreads)
- **Layout:** Each "spread" shows left page, flip for right page
- **Fonts to Use:**
  - Title: Fredoka One, Quicksand, or Baloo (rounded, playful)
  - Body: Open Sans or Nunito (readable)
- **Color Palette:**
  - Soft Blue: #A0C4FF
  - Warm Cream: #FFF8E7
  - Navy Text: #2D3748
  - Golden Accent: #ECC94B
- **Art Style:** Soft watercolor, non-scary, cozy
