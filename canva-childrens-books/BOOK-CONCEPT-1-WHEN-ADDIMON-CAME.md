# Children's Book Concept #1: "When Addimon Came"
## Trauma-Informed Children's Book Series | Canva-Ready Pages

---

## BOOK OVERVIEW

**Character:** Addimon - A mysterious, shadowy figure (not scary, more like a fog/wind)
**Perspective:** Child narrator (Taylor, age 10)
**Mood:** Gentle, validating, hopeful - never scary
**Age Range:** 4-8 years old (picture book)

---

## PAGE-BY-PAGE BREAKDOWN

### PAGE 1: COVER
**Visual:** Child's bedroom at night, soft moonlight. A gentle purple/blue fog creeps under the door. The child (Taylor) is tucked in bed, looking curious not scared.
**Text:** "When Addimon Came"
**Subtitle:** "A story about the tricky visitor"
**Design Notes:** Warm bedspread, nightlight glow, Addimon shown as swirling mist not a monster

### PAGE 2: Title Page
**Visual:** Taylor introducing herself, sitting on her bed with her stuffed bunny
**Text:** "When Addimon Came"
**Text:** "By Taylor"
**Design Notes:** Personal, warm - photo frame style

### PAGE 3: The First Time
**Visual:** Taylor in the living room,Mom/Dad acting strange (glassy eyes, falling asleep on couch). Taylor looks confused, not terrified.
**Text:** "I remember the first time Addimon visited our house. Mom was sitting on the couch, but she wasn't really there. Her eyes looked like TV when it shows snow. I asked her a question and she didn't answer. I thought maybe I was invisible."
**Design Notes:** Soft focus on adult, Taylor slightly blurred but clear emotion

### PAGE 4: What is Addimon?
**Visual:** Addimon as friendly-ish shadow figure (like fog with eyes) peeking through window
**Text:** "Addimon is a tricky visitor. You can't see Addimon with a telescope or a microscope. Addimon sneaks in when nobody's looking. Addimon makes people forget about dinner, and bedtime, and me."
**Design Notes:** Non-scary, more mysterious. Use soft purples/blues

### PAGE 5: It's Not Your Fault
**Visual:** Taylor in her room, hugging bunny, with Addimon's fog at the door. But Taylor is looking strong.
**Text:** "Sometimes kids think: Did I do something bad? Is it my fault? But the answer is NO. Addimon was there before you. You didn't bring Addimon. And you can't make Addimon leave."
**Design Notes:** Empowering posture, warm colors in Taylor's room

### PAGE 6: The Feelings Song
**Visual:** Taylor with big feelings shown as colorful bubbles - sad (blue), mad (red), confused (purple)
**Text:** "When Addimon visits, lots of feelings come. You might feel sad, or mad, or scared, or confused. All feelings are okay. Even big feelings fit in your heart."
**Design Notes:** Bright, friendly bubble graphics

### PAGE 7: Finding the Grown-Ups
**Visual:** Taylor reaching out to a safe adult (grandma, teacher, neighbor)
**Text:** "It's okay to tell a grown-up what you see. You can say: 'I need help.' There are safe people who love you. You don't have to carry Addimon's visits alone."
**Design Notes:** Warm connection between child and adult

### PAGE 8: Your Safe Place
**Visual:** Taylor in her "safe place" - a cozy corner with bunny, books, blanket
**Text:** "Every kid needs a safe place. Maybe it's your bed with the covers over your head. Maybe it's grandma's house. Maybe it's a big tree outside. Find YOUR safe place and remember: you are safe there."
**Design Notes:** Cozy, inviting, soft colors

### PAGE 9: You're Not Alone
**Visual:** Taylor with other kids - showing community
**Text:** "Do you know other kids who have an Addimon visitor? You're not alone. Lots of kids have tricky visitors in their houses. You are brave. You are good. You are not alone."
**Design Notes:** Diverse group of happy kids

### PAGE 10: The Morning After
**Visual:** Morning light, Addimon gone (or leaving), Taylor looking out window toward sunrise
**Text:** "Addimon doesn't stay forever. Sometimes visits end. Sometimes they come back. But here's what I know: The sun comes up. Morning comes. And I am still here."
**Design Notes:** Hopeful, warm sunrise colors

### PAGE 11: For the Grown-Ups
**Visual:** Simple page with resource info
**Text:** "For the grown-ups: This book is for children who live with addiction. Resources inside. You're doing great."
**Design Notes:** Clean, minimal - parent resource page

### PAGE 12: Back Cover
**Visual:** Taylor with her bunny, waving
**Text:** "Through Taylor's Tears"
**Tagline:** "Every child's feeling is valid"
**Design Notes:** Warm, inviting

## CANVA SETUP NOTES

- **Page Size:** 8.5 x 11" (standard picture book)
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary: Soft Navy (#4A5568)
  - Secondary: Warm Purple (#9F7AEA)
  - Accent: Amber/Gold (#ECC94B)
  - Text: Dark Charcoal (#2D3748)
  - Backgrounds: Cream (#FFFAF0)
- **Fonts to Use:**
  - Title: Rounded, friendly (like Fredoka One or similar)
  - Body: Readable, warm (like Nunito or Open Sans)
- **Art Style:** Soft watercolor or digital illustration, warm and non-threatening
