# Children's Book Concept #1: "When Addimon Came"

## BOOK OVERVIEW

- **Character:** Addimon (shadow/fog creature)
- **Perspective:** Child narrator (Taylor)
- **Rhyme Scheme:** AABB, 2-4 lines per spread
- **Age Range:** 4-8 years old

### SPREAD 3: What is Addimon?
**Page 3 (Left):** [Illustration - Addimon as friendly shadow/fog]
**Page 3 (Right):**
```
Addimon's tricky, he's sneaky, he's sly,

```

### SPREAD 4: What Addimon Does
**Page 4 (Left):** [Illustration - parent acting strange]
