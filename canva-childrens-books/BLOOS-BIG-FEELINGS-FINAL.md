# Bloo's Big Feelings
## A Children's Book About Emotions

---

In a meadow soft and bright,
Lived a bunny, small and light.
Bloo had fur like twilight skies,
And curious, wide, and wondering eyes.

One calm morning, still and slow,
Bloo felt something start to grow.
Not quite happy, not quite sad,
Just a feeling strange and bad.

His chest felt tight, his nose would twitch,
"What is this?"—inside a switch.
"I don't like this," Bloo said aloud,
As the feeling grew more big and loud.

So off he hopped without delay,
To find out what he felt that day.

---

## 😡 Anger – The Stomping Hill

Soon the ground began to shake—
STOMP, STOMP, STOMP with every quake!
Heat rushed quickly through Bloo's toes,
Up his ears and to his nose.

"I want to yell! I want to shout!
I want this feeling OUT, OUT, OUT!"

A Bear appeared with steady gaze,
"That's anger's fire, its sudden blaze."

"But it's too big!" cried Bloo in fear.
"Big, yes," said Bear, "but listen here."

"Plant your feet upon the ground,
Breathe in slow… don't rush around."

In… and out… so calm, so deep,
The fire slowed its angry heap.

"Oh," said Bloo, "it's not so loud…"
The stomping faded from the ground.

"This feeling is here… but it won't stay.
I can breathe, and be okay."

---

## 😢 Sadness – The Quiet Rain

Drip… drop… plop… the sky turned gray,
The light and warmth just slipped away.

Bloo felt heavy, small, and low,
With nowhere bright or fast to go.
His ears drooped down, his steps were slow,
"I don't feel like myself no more."

A gentle Deer stood in the rain,
"This feeling comes with quiet pain."

"It's sadness," Deer said soft and kind,
"A way to ease your heart and mind."

"But I don't like it," Bloo replied.
"You don't have to," Deer said, "just don't hide."

"You can cry or sit awhile,
Let it pass, just be yourself a while."

A tear fell down… then one… then two…
The heavy feeling slowly flew.

The clouds grew thin, the sky grew light,
The world felt soft again, not tight.

"This feeling is here… but it won't stay.
I can breathe, and be okay."

---

## 😨 Fear – The Whispering Dark

The forest dimmed, the light grew thin,
Long shadows reached and pulled Bloo in.

A SNAP! a rustle—something near—
Bloo froze in place, "I feel… fear…"

His heart beat fast, his paws felt cold,
The night felt big, too dark, too bold.

"What if something's hiding there?"
"What if danger's everywhere?"

An Owl swooped down with steady grace,
"I see the worry on your face."

"Fear helps keep you safe and aware,
But not all thoughts mean danger's there."

"Then what can I do?" asked Bloo, unsure.
"Let's find what's real, and what's pure."

"Name what you see, and feel, and hear…"
"The trees… the ground… your voice is near…"

"Now take a breath—both slow and deep…"
The racing heart began to sleep.

The dark stayed dark—but softer now,
No longer something Bloo must fight somehow.

"This feeling is here… but it won't stay.
I can breathe, and be okay."

---

## 😤 Frustration – The Tangled Path

Bloo soon reached a tangled trail,
With twisted vines that would not fail
To trip and tug and block the way,
No matter how Bloo tried to stay.

"I can't do it!" Bloo cried out,
Stuck in loops of twist and doubt.

A Beaver gnawed nearby with cheer,
"You seem frustrated—that is clear."

"Frustration comes when things feel tough,
When trying hard is not enough."

"Then what should I do?" Bloo said with strain.
"Pause," said Beaver, "then try again."

"Take a break, then start anew,
Small steps first—that's what you do."

Bloo stepped back, then tried once more,
And found a path not seen before.

"Oh!" said Bloo, "I made it through!"
"You did," said Beaver, "because you grew."

"This feeling is here… but it won't stay.
I can breathe, and be okay."

---

## 😳 Embarrassment – The Blushing Clearing

Bloo then tripped—oh what a sight!
Right in a clearing, big and bright.

A Flamingo blinked and looked his way,
Bloo wished he could just run away.

His cheeks felt warm, his ears bent low,
"I feel so silly… everyone knows…"

Flamingo smiled, calm and kind,
"Embarrassment plays tricks on the mind."

"It tells you all are watching you,
But that's not always really true."

"Even if they saw," she said with grace,
"We all make slips—it's part of the race."

Bloo gave a laugh, small but real,
The warmth began to gently heal.

"It wasn't so bad," Bloo said with a grin,
"I can still stand tall within."

"This feeling is here… but it won't stay.
I can breathe, and be okay."

---

## 😄 Joy – The Golden Meadow

At last, a meadow filled with light,
Warm and glowing, soft and bright.

A Fox ran past with playful cheer,
"Joy has found you—yes, it's here!"

Bloo felt light from head to toe,
A happy warmth began to grow.

He laughed and leapt and twirled around,
Joy danced freely all around.

"This feeling's lovely," Bloo said wide,
"I wish that it would always stay inside!"

Fox just smiled and shook his head,
"All feelings come and go," they said.

"Joy is special, bright, and true—
But it's not meant to stay with you."

Bloo stood still and thought it through,
"All my feelings help me too…"

"This feeling is here… but it won't stay.
I can breathe, and be okay."

---

## 🌈 Ending

Back in the meadow, calm and bright,
Bloo rested in the morning light.

"I felt them all—both big and small,
And now I'm not afraid at all."

"My feelings come, my feelings go,
Like waves that ebb, like winds that blow."

"I don't need to hide or fight,
I can feel… and be alright."

With steady heart and gentle sway,
Bloo smiled softly at the day.

And whenever feelings call,
Bloo remembers: I'm okay through it all.
