# Research Summary: Children of Addicted Parents
## Compiled: April 2, 2026

---

## 📊 KEY STATISTICS

- **1 in 4 children** grow up in a home with adults with Substance Use Disorders (SUDs)
- **46.3 million** individuals aged 12+ in the US have a substance use disorder
- Many are parents, creating a cycle that affects generations
- Children of alcoholics (COAs) are at higher risk for:
  - Developing substance use disorders themselves
  - Mental health challenges
  - Relationship difficulties
  - Behavioral problems
