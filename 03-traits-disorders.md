# Personality Traits & Disorders in Adult Children of Addicts
## Research-Based Findings

## 🧠 THE "LAUNDRY LIST" - 14 Common Traits
(Source: Adult Children of Alcoholics - ACA Fellowship)

### The Original 14 Traits:

1. **We became reactor personalities** - learned to react rather than act intentionally
2. **We learned to view life as a crisis** - always expecting the worst, can't relax
3. **We have an overdeveloped sense of responsibility** - feel guilty for things beyond our control
4. **We are denial specialists** - minimize, rationalize, justify problems
5. **We learned to check our emotional-libido at the door** - learned not to feel, don't know what we feel
6. **We learned to live in chaos** - unstable environments feel "normal," stability feels wrong
7. **We learned to be dependent** - terrified of abandonment, will do anything to hold onto relationships
8. **We are mixed-up people** - introversion + extroversion, confused about identity
9. **We are critical and self-critical** - perfectionists, harsh on ourselves and others
10. **We learned to respond to anyone else's creative force** - people-pleasing, difficulty saying no
11. **We are easily离心了** - have difficulty with intimate relationships (too close or too distant)
12. **We have difficulty with intimacy** - can share superficially but not deeply
13. **We learned to fear authority** - and sometimes are attracted to it
14. **We are subject to temporal shifts** - living in the past or future, not present

## 🔬 RESEARCH-BASED PERSONALITY FINDINGS

### From PMC/Clinical Studies:

**1. IMPULSIVITY/DISINHIBITION** (Most Consistent Finding)
- Sensation seeking
- Aggressiveness
- Difficulty with self-control
- Risk-taking behaviors

**2. DIFFICULTY WITH EMOTIONAL REGULATION**
- Can't identify emotions (alexithymia)
- Explosive outbursts OR total shutdown
- "Emotional flashbacks" - minor disagreement feels like crisis

**3. ATTACHMENT ISSUES**
- Anxious attachment style
- Fear of abandonment
- Attracted to unavailable people
- Repeat unhealthy relationship patterns

**4. PERFECTIONISM**
- Compulsive need to be perfect
- Low self-esteem despite achievements
- "If I do everything right, maybe they'll love me"
- Burnout from over-giving

## 🩺 CLINICAL DISORDERS COMMONLY SEEN

### Personality Disorders (Higher Rates in ACOA Population):

**1. Borderline Personality Disorder (BPD)**
- Instability in relationships
- Identity disturbance
- Impulsive behaviors
- Emotional dysregulation
- Chronic feelings of emptiness

**2. Avoidant Personality Disorder**
- Social inhibition
- Feelings of inadequacy
- Hypersensitivity to criticism
- Avoids occupational activities requiring contact

**3. Dependent Personality Disorder**
- Excessive need to be taken care of
- Submissive behavior
- Difficulty making decisions without reassurance
- Fears abandonment

**4. Antisocial Tendencies**
- Some studies show higher rates
- May manifest as rebellion, authority issues
- Difficulty with rules/laws

## 😔 COMMON MENTAL HEALTH CHALLENGES

### Depression
- High rates among ACOAs
- Learned helplessness
- "Nothing I do matters"

### Anxiety Disorders
- Generalized anxiety
- Social anxiety
- Panic attacks
- Always "on edge"

### PTSD/C-PTSD
- Complex trauma from prolonged exposure
- "Emotional flashbacks"
- Triggered by seemingly small things

### Substance Use Disorders
- Higher genetic predisposition
- Self-medication with drugs/alcohol
- 2-4x more likely to develop addiction

### Eating Disorders
- Control issues - food becomes something they can control
- Common in those who had no control in childhood

## 💪 SURVIVAL MECHANISMS THAT BECOME PROBLEMS

| Childhood Survival Skill | Adult Problem |
|-------------------------|---------------|
| Monitoring parent's mood | Hypervigilance in relationships |
| Being quiet to stay safe | Suppressing needs, people-pleasing |
| Taking care of siblings | Over-giving, burnout, resentment |
| Knowing what triggers parent | Anxiety, difficulty relaxing |
| Hiding the truth | Trust issues, secret-keeping |
| Being invisible | Difficulty with self-promotion, feeling unseen |
| Being perfect | Perfectionism, people-pleasing |
| Solving problems alone | Difficulty asking for help |

---

## ✨ WHAT HELPS (HEALING FACTORS)

- Understanding the family disease
- 12-step programs (ACA, Al-Anon)
- Therapy (trauma-informed)
- Finding community of others who understand
- Learning to identify and name emotions
- Setting boundaries
- "Inner child" work
- Learning it's NOT their fault
